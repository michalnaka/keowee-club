September 24–27

This weekend on the lakes.

Walhalla PAC is dark this week — nothing on its calendar until Larry Fleet on the 30th — so Seneca carries the weekend instead. Jazz on the Alley owns Thursday like always, and the SC Foothills Heritage Fair runs its final three days at the Farm Center. Saturday's the busy one: Keowee Brewing goes full Oktoberfest, Rock the Ranch throws a music festival out on Charlie B Farm Road, and Jocassee Lake Tours has a Full Harvest Moon cruise at sunset. Both breweries keep their regular live music going right through Sunday.

Thursday · September 24Seneca

Jazz on the Alley featuring SONG

Seneca's Thursday-night institution keeps rolling through October: free live jazz on historic Ram Cat Alley. This week's main act is the four-piece band SONG, with the Happy Hour Showcase warming up the street beforehand.

Free · Happy Hour Showcase 5:30–6:30pm, main set 7–9pm · Ram Cat Alley, downtown Seneca

Thu–Sat · September 24–26Seneca

SC Foothills Heritage Fair

The Farm Center's fair wraps up its run this week — rides, exhibits, and fair food on Sandifer Blvd. Thursday is spirit night for West-Oak area schools, with free admission for kids in school colors when paired with a paid adult.

$10 adults · $5 students · under 6 free · Thu 4–10pm, Fri 4–11pm, Sat 10am–11pm · The Farm Center, 2063 Sandifer Blvd, Seneca

Friday · September 25Breweries

Free live music at both breweries

Keowee Brewing has Halem Albright playing Friday night in Seneca. Over in Salem, Jocassee Valley Brewing has Fayssoux McLean & Brandon Turner with the Squeals on Wheels food truck out front. Both are free and easy to wander into after dinner.

Free · Keowee Brewing 6:30–8:30pm · Jocassee Valley Brewing music 6:30pm, food truck from 4pm

Check details & availability ↗

Saturday · September 26Seneca

Oktoberfest at Keowee Brewing

Keowee Brewing turns its whole Saturday into an Oktoberfest — a longer-than-usual day at the taproom on Main Street. If you'd rather have a quieter night, Jocassee Valley Brewing has Spalding McIntosh at 6:30pm instead, with a Caribbean food truck.

11am–10pm · Keowee Brewing, 401 E Main St, Seneca · check the taproom for the full lineup

Check details & availability ↗

Saturday · September 26Seneca

Rock the Ranch Music Festival

A music festival out on Charlie B Farm Road. The festival's own site hadn't posted set times as of this writing, so call ahead or check their page before you drive out.

155 Charlie B Farm Rd, Seneca · check the operator for times & tickets

Check details & availability ↗

Saturday · September 26Salem

Full Harvest Moon tour with Jocassee Lake Tours

A slow evening boat ride timed to catch both the sunset and the full moon rising over Lake Jocassee, with a waterfall or two along the way. Full-moon nights book up, so reserve ahead.

5:45–7:45pm · Jocassee Lake Tours, Salem, SC · reserve online or call 864-280-5501

Check details & availability ↗

Sunday · September 27Salem

Live music closes the weekend at Jocassee Valley Brewing

The one thing confirmed for Sunday: Matt Phillips & What's Good playing an early-evening set, with Farm to Fire Fermentery pizza out front. A calmer way to end the weekend than Saturday's options.

Free · Music at 4pm · open 12–7pm · Jocassee Valley Brewing, Salem
