August 27–30

This weekend on the lakes.

The Cleverlys bring their Ozark bluegrass-comedy to Walhalla Friday night, a full moon rises over Jocassee the same evening, and Jazz on the Alley still owns Ram Cat Alley on Thursday — while Jocassee Valley Brewing stacks four straight nights of live music through Sunday.

Thursday · August 27Seneca

Jazz on the Alley

Seneca's Thursday-night institution — free live jazz on historic Ram Cat Alley, every week through October. The happy-hour set starts at 5:30, the main act at 6:30. Get dinner downtown and let the weekend start a day early.

Free · 6:30–9pm (happy hour 5:30) · Ram Cat Alley, downtown Seneca

Check details & availability ↗

Friday · August 28Walhalla

The Cleverlys

A bluegrass band out of the remote Ozarks that plays it dead straight and completely absurd at the same time — sharp picking wrapped around deadpan comedy. One night only at the old Walhalla auditorium.

7:30pm · $32–$52 · Walhalla Performing Arts Center

Check details & availability ↗

Friday · August 28Lake Jocassee

Full Moon Rising Tour

Same easy, slow-moving boat as the Saturday sunset run, timed instead to watch the moon come up full over Jocassee Dam as the sky goes dark. A waterfall or two along the way, as always.

$65 · 2 hours · Jocassee Lake Tours

Check details & availability ↗

Saturday · August 29Lake Jocassee

Sunset Lake Tour

A slower two-hour run out onto Jocassee as the light goes gold over the mountains, with a waterfall or two along the way. Runs every Saturday evening through the season.

$65 · 2 hours · every Saturday · Jocassee Lake Tours

Check details & availability ↗

Sunday · August 30Lake Jocassee

Snorkel Adventure Tour

New this year — a guided look at what's actually swimming around in Jocassee's clear water, fish and invertebrates included. Longer than the boat tours, and good for kids 4 and up.

$125 · 6 hours · ages 4+ · Jocassee Lake Tours

Check details & availability ↗

Thu–Sun · August 27–30Breweries

Four nights of live music at Jocassee Valley

Jocassee Valley Brewing in Salem has a full lineup posted: Old Time Jam Thursday, The Binge Friday, Done Gone Boys Saturday, and Jazzy Trinity closing it out Sunday afternoon, each with a food truck alongside. Keowee Brewing in Seneca runs its usual free Friday and Saturday music too — check its calendar for who's on.

Free · sets start 6:30pm Thu–Sat, 4pm Sun at Jocassee Valley · Keowee Brewing Fri & Sat, lineup TBD
