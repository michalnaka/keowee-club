5 verified picks

A short list on purpose. Availability and weather can move faster than this page, so check the organizer before pointing the car north.

Thursday · August 13Seneca

Jazz on the Alley

Seneca's Thursday-night institution — free live jazz on historic Ram Cat Alley, every week through October. The happy-hour set starts at 5:30, the main act at 6:30. Get dinner downtown and let the weekend start a day early.

Free · 6:30–9pm (happy hour 5:30) · Ram Cat Alley, downtown Seneca

Check details & availability ↗

Friday · August 14Walhalla

Sweet Baby James — James Taylor Tribute

Bill Griese brings the Fire and Rain / You've Got a Friend / Steamroller catalog to the old Walhalla auditorium for the venue's own James Taylor night. Big-room sound, twenty minutes from the water.

7:30pm · $39–$57 · Walhalla Performing Arts Center

Check details & availability ↗

Friday · August 14Lake Jocassee

Jocassee Family Adventure Camp — last one of the year

The every-Friday, all-ages tour of caves, waterfalls, and salamander hunting wraps its season with this date. It needs six people to run and seats are limited, so book ahead if you want it.

Ages 16–64 $75 · Ages 4–15 $70 · 3 & under free with a paid adult · park entry separate

Check details & availability ↗

Fri & Sat · August 14–15Breweries

Live music at both lake breweries

Keowee Brewing in Seneca and Jocassee Valley Brewing in Salem both run free live music Friday and Saturday nights, with food trucks at Jocassee Valley. Neither calendar had this week's acts posted as of publish — check the links before you head out.

Free · Fri & Sat · check each calendar for this week's lineup and set times

Check details & availability ↗

Sunday · August 16Lake Jocassee

Wild River Adventure Tour

A six-hour push into the gorges by boat — hidden waterfalls, swimming holes, and an old-growth rainforest pocket you can't reach any other way. The operator is upfront that it's for the physically fit.

$125 · 6 hours · ages 10+ · Jocassee Lake Tours
