September 17–20

This weekend on the lakes.

This is the biggest stretch Walhalla PAC has run all year: a Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young tribute Friday, Marty Stuart & His Fabulous Superlatives Saturday, and Jason Crabb's gospel show Sunday — three nights, three different crowds. Saturday during the day belongs to Walhalla itself, with the Hispanic Heritage Festival at Owens Park and the countywide Fall Farm Crawl running at the same time. Jazz on the Alley still owns Thursday downtown, and both breweries keep their usual Friday–Saturday music going.

Thursday · September 17Seneca

Jazz on the Alley featuring Jamie Wright

Seneca's Thursday-night institution — free live jazz on historic Ram Cat Alley, every week through October. This week's main act is Jamie Wright, with the Happy Hour Showcase ahead of her set. Get dinner downtown and let the weekend start a day early.

Free · Happy Hour Showcase 5:30–6:30pm, main set 7–9pm · Ram Cat Alley, downtown Seneca

Friday · September 18Walhalla

Harvest Moon: A Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young Tribute at Walhalla PAC

The Performing Arts Center opens a three-night run with a tribute act built around CSNY's harmonies — a good seated-show anchor if you want dinner downtown first and a real evening out.

7:30pm · Walhalla Performing Arts Center · check the venue for remaining tickets & pricing

Friday · September 18Seneca

Blue Ridge Arts Center opening reception

New exhibit opening in downtown Seneca — the Blue Ridge Photographic Society's show, up through late October. The reception is free and open to the public; pairs easily with dinner on Ram Cat Alley beforehand.

Free · 5:30–7pm · Blue Ridge Arts Center, Seneca

Saturday · September 19Walhalla

Hispanic Heritage Festival

The weekend's flagship free event: a full day of mariachi, folklorico dancing, salsa and norteño, and a DJ finale at Owens Park. The city's posted a full schedule of acts — worth building the day around if you're bringing kids.

Free · 11am–6pm · Owens Park, Walhalla

Saturday · September 19Oconee County

2026 Fall Farm Crawl

Self-guided drive between local farms — pick your own order, stay as long as you want at each stop. Check in at Chattooga Belle Farm, Walhalla PAC, or Lifeline Community Church in Westminster to get your wristband and map.

$12/person (4 & under free) · 9am–5pm, check-in 8:30–10am · three check-in locations countywide

Marty Stuart & His Fabulous Superlatives at Walhalla PAC

The best ticket of the weekend: a genuine country-and-bluegrass legend playing a small-town arts center. If you're only picking one night at the PAC this week, this is probably it.

7:30pm · Walhalla Performing Arts Center · check the venue for remaining tickets & pricing

Jason Crabb at Walhalla PAC

The PAC closes its three-night run with an evening of gospel from the award-winning Christian artist. A quieter way to end the weekend than the last two nights.

Fri–Sat · September 18–19Breweries

Free live music at both breweries

Jocassee Valley Brewing in Salem has Big Eyes Parker playing Friday and Crank Dogs Saturday, with food trucks alongside. Keowee Brewing in Seneca has Jeff Holley Friday and BJ Callihan Saturday. All four sets are free and run 6:30–8:30pm.

Free · Fri & Sat, 6:30–8:30pm · Jeff Holley & BJ Callihan at Keowee · Big Eyes Parker & Crank Dogs at Jocassee Valley
