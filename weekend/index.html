October 8–11

This weekend on the lakes.

Jazz on the Alley names its act this time: Nathan Angelo plays Ram Cat Alley Thursday. From there the weekend gets crowded in a good way — Hannah Harper plays two nights at Walhalla PAC, Westminster throws its annual Bigfoot Festival, and Oconee County runs its Veterans Appreciation Festival Saturday. Both breweries keep their usual Friday–Saturday music going underneath all of it, and Jocassee Valley Brewing closes things out Sunday afternoon.

6 verified picks

A short list on purpose. Availability and weather can move faster than this page, so check the organizer before pointing the car north.

Thursday · October 8Seneca

Jazz on the Alley: Nathan Angelo

The city's October lineup is out, and this week's headliner is Nathan Angelo. Same free-and-easy setup as always — the Happy Hour Showcase warms up the street, then the main act takes over.

Free · Happy Hour Showcase 5:30–6:30pm, Nathan Angelo 7–9pm · Ram Cat Alley, downtown Seneca

Friday · October 9Westminster

SC Bigfoot Festival

Westminster's main street turns over to Bigfoot for the weekend — live music, vendors, Sasquatch-themed competitions, speakers, and kid stuff, running Friday into Saturday. It's a drive from the lake, but it's the kind of unabashedly weird regional event worth the trip once.

Free to attend · Fri–Sat, times TBD — check the operator · Main Street, Westminster

Check details & availability ↗

Friday · October 9Walhalla

Hannah Harper, two nights at Walhalla PAC

The rising country act plays back-to-back shows Friday and Saturday — same set time both nights. Walhalla PAC's room is small enough that it never feels like a big-venue show, which is most of its appeal.

$59–$79 · 7:30pm both nights · Walhalla Performing Arts Center, 101 E. North Broad St, Walhalla

Check details & availability ↗

Saturday · October 10Oconee County

Veterans Appreciation Festival & Ride of Honor

Oconee County's 5th annual Veterans Festival adds a first this year: the Ride of Honor, a motorcycle ride held the same day. Start time and route weren't posted when we checked — the festival's Facebook page is the best source before you head out.

Check the operator for times · Oconee County

Saturday · October 10Breweries

Brewery music carries on underneath it all

If Bigfoot and the Veterans Festival aren't your Saturday, both breweries run their usual live-music night: Jocassee Valley Brewing has Alma Russ with the Pachuys food truck, and Keowee Brewing has its regular Friday–Saturday set (check their calendar for who's playing — it wasn't posted by name when we checked).

Free · Jocassee Valley Brewing music 6:30pm · Keowee Brewing music 6:30–8:30pm

Sunday · October 11Salem

Jocassee Valley Brewing closes the weekend

Marshall Goers and Lucy Allen play an early-evening set with Majoes Filipino food out front — a calm landing after a loud Saturday.

Free · Music at 4pm · open 12–7pm · Jocassee Valley Brewing, Salem
