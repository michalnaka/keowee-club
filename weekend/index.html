October 1–4

This weekend on the lakes.

Jazz on the Alley keeps its Thursday residency in Ram Cat Alley — the city hadn't posted an October lineup as of this writing, so it's a surprise headliner this week. Both breweries carry the weekend from there: Keowee Brewing has Amy Kincaid Friday and Angela Easterling Saturday, plus a First Friday Fish & Chips special. Jocassee Valley Brewing goes bigger on Saturday with its own Octobeerfest — German food, the PaddleSplash Awards at noon, and music into the evening — then closes Sunday with West End Strings. Walhalla PAC's and Visit Oconee's own event pages were blocking automated access when we checked, so we're leaving anything unverified off this week's list rather than guess.

Thursday · October 1Seneca

Jazz on the Alley

Seneca's Thursday-night institution keeps going through October: free live jazz on historic Ram Cat Alley, with the Happy Hour Showcase warming up the street beforehand. The city's flyer confirms the series runs every Thursday through October, but hadn't named this week's headliner as of this writing — check the page below before you head down.

Free · Happy Hour Showcase 5:30–6:30pm, main set 7–9pm · Ram Cat Alley, downtown Seneca · this week's act wasn't posted yet

Friday · October 2Breweries

First Friday specials and live music at both breweries

Keowee Brewing runs a First Friday Fish & Chips special most of the day and has Amy Kincaid playing that evening. Over in Salem, Jocassee Valley Brewing has Chris Wayne with the Just a Smile Caribbean food truck out front.

Free · Keowee Brewing Fish & Chips 11am–9pm, music 6:30–8:30pm · Jocassee Valley Brewing music 6:30pm, food truck from 4pm

Saturday · October 3Salem

Octobeerfest at Jocassee Valley Brewing

Jocassee Valley Brewing's own spin on Oktoberfest — German food from multiple vendors, the PaddleSplash Awards at noon, and music running Water Kickers in the early afternoon into Jaguar 4 that evening.

German food & vendors 11am–7pm · PaddleSplash Awards 12pm · Water Kickers 1:30pm, Jaguar 4 6:30pm · Jocassee Valley Brewing, Salem
