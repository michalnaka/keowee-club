5 verified picks

A short list on purpose. Availability and weather can move faster than this page, so check the organizer before pointing the car north.

Thursday · August 6Seneca

Jazz on the Alley

Seneca's Thursday-night institution — free live jazz on historic Ram Cat Alley, every week through October. The happy-hour set starts at 5:30, the main act at 6:30. Get dinner downtown and let the weekend start a day early.

Free · 6:30–9pm (happy hour 5:30) · Ram Cat Alley, downtown Seneca

Check details & availability ↗

Friday · August 7Walhalla

Ally Venable Band at WPAC

Texas blues-rock guitar in the old Walhalla auditorium — a big-room show twenty minutes from the water. Doors at the Performing Arts Center, show at 7:30pm.

7:30pm · $37–$57 · Walhalla Performing Arts Center

Check details & availability ↗

Friday · August 7Lake Jocassee

Jocassee Family Adventure Camp

The every-Friday, all-ages tour of waterfalls, swimming holes, and salamander habitat that makes the ride home interesting. Limited seats.

Adults $75 · Ages 4–15 $70 · Park entry separate — confirm with the operator

Check details & availability ↗

Saturday · August 8Walhalla

Corey Smith — Southern Drawl Tour

Georgia country singer-songwriter at the Walhalla Performing Arts Center. If your lake day ends early, this is the Saturday night.

7:30pm · $49–$69 · Walhalla Performing Arts Center

Check details & availability ↗

Fri & Sat · August 7–8Breweries

Live music at both lake breweries

Keowee Brewing in Seneca has free live music every Friday and Saturday, and Jocassee Valley Brewing in Salem runs bands and food trucks both nights. Lineups post on their calendars.

Free at KBC · check each calendar for who's playing
