November 13–15

This weekend on the lakes.

Walhalla Performing Arts Center stacks two heavy hitters back to back — Sammy Kershaw Friday, Sam Bush Saturday — while Jocassee Valley Brewing runs music and food trucks all three nights, opening Friday with a brook trout fireside chat before the band.
