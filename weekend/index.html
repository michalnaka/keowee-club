November 27–29

This weekend on the lakes.

Thanksgiving weekend brings two shows to Walhalla Performing Arts Center — a Fleetwood Mac tribute Friday and Celtic Angels Christmas Sunday — plus a window-decorating contest and a little man-made snow downtown, a holiday market and workshops at the Seneca Treehouse Project, and both lakeside breweries running their usual Friday-through-Sunday music and food trucks.

Friday · November 27Walhalla

Rumours: A Fleetwood Mac Tribute Returns 2026

The long-running Fleetwood Mac tribute act plays the Walhalla Performing Arts Center stage for a post-Thanksgiving night out. Note: some ticket resellers list this wrong — the venue's own calendar confirms Friday.

$57–$77 · 7:30pm · Walhalla Performing Arts Center

Friday · November 27Walhalla

Window decorating contest and a little snow, downtown Walhalla

Downtown businesses deck out their storefronts for the City of Walhalla's holiday window contest, with community leaders judging and prizes for the winners. Up the street at the Sunni Ann Mercantile, "It's Snowing at the Mercantile" brings artificial snowfall, giveaways, and specials for a half hour.

Snow event free, 1–1:30pm · window contest all day, downtown Walhalla

Check details & availability ↗

Friday & Saturday · November 27 & 28Seneca

Free live music Friday and Saturday at Keowee Brewing

Keowee Brewing runs live music both nights, rain or shine, like clockwork — the lineup posts to their Facebook events page closer to showtime rather than the website, so check there or ask at the bar.

Free · 6:30–8:30pm Fri & Sat · Keowee Brewing, 401 E. Main St., Seneca

Check details & availability ↗

Friday–Sunday · November 27–29Salem

Weekend music and food trucks at Jocassee Valley Brewing

Closed Thanksgiving Day, Jocassee Valley Brewing reopens Friday at noon with Spalding McIntosh (Caribbean) and Just A Smile's truck, Saturday brings West End Strings and Buns and Bones, and Sunday closes with Jay Maybry (country) and Farm to Fire Fermentery's wood-fired pizza.

Free to attend · Fri 12–9pm, Sat 9am–9pm, Sun 12–7pm · Jocassee Valley Brewing, Salem

Check details & availability ↗

Saturday · November 28Seneca

Green Saturday holiday market and make-n-take workshops

The Seneca Treehouse Project's holiday market is free to browse — handmade gifts and vendors — with ticketed make-n-take workshops (charcuterie boards, infused oil rollers, intention candles) and kids' activities alongside it. Check the event page for the workshop schedule and the VIP All Access Pass.

Market free · workshops ticketed · Seneca Treehouse Project
