Thursday · September 3Seneca

Jazz on the Alley

Seneca's Thursday-night institution — free live jazz on historic Ram Cat Alley, every week through October. The happy-hour set starts at 5:30, the main act at 6:30. Get dinner downtown and let the weekend start a day early.

Free · 6:30–9pm (happy hour 5:30) · Ram Cat Alley, downtown Seneca

Saturday · September 5Lake Jocassee

Sunset Lake Tour

A slower two-hour run out onto Jocassee as the light goes gold over the mountains, with a waterfall or two along the way. Runs every Saturday evening through the season.

$65 · 2 hours · every Saturday · Jocassee Lake Tours

Fri–Sun · September 4–6Breweries

Three nights of live music at Jocassee Valley

Jocassee Valley Brewing in Salem has its lineup posted: Kodesh BBQ & Bowls and According to Taste on Friday, Squeals on Wheels with Finkelstein Three on Saturday, and Farm to Fire pizza with Suze Luna & Dick Wells closing it out Sunday afternoon. Keowee Brewing in Seneca runs its usual free Friday and Saturday music too — check its calendar for who's on.

Free · sets start 6:30pm Fri & Sat, 4pm Sun at Jocassee Valley · Keowee Brewing Fri & Sat, lineup on their calendar
