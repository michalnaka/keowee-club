Where to swim around the lake

The public beaches and swim spots on Keowee and Jocassee — where the buoys are, where the water's warm, what it costs to park, and the live lake level before you pack the towels.

Lake Keowee

4 swim spots

No lifeguards anywhere on the lake — everything here is swim-at-your-own-risk, and the bottom drops off fast in places (see exactly how fast on the depth map).

High Falls County Park

SenecaBeachBuoyed swim area

The most official swim on Keowee — a mostly level beach at the end of the park with a dedicated swimming area marked by buoys that keep boats out. Picnic shelters, playground, and camping steps away.

Mile Creek County Park

Six MileBeachWarm cove

The family favorite — a shallow, protected cove that warms up fast on sunny afternoons, with a playground right beside the beach. About $5 per car (bring cash).

South Cove County Park

SenecaBeachCamping

A popular sandy beach near the deepest end of the lake — worth knowing that Oconee County doesn't formally designate it as a swimming area, so it's officially at-your-own-risk. $3 per vehicle at the gate.

Keowee-Toxaway State Park

SunsetNatural shorelineTrails

No beach here — this is natural-shoreline swimming off the rocks and coves along the trails, on the quiet northern end of Keowee. Come for a hike-and-dip day, not an umbrella-and-cooler day.

Lake Jocassee

1 public access

The clearest water in South Carolina — and all of it reached through one gate.

Devils Fork State Park

SalemClear waterShore swimming

Jocassee's only public access. There's no formal swim beach, but small sandy shoreline areas near the day-use side are family favorites, and the gin-clear water is the whole point — this is the closest thing to Caribbean swimming in the Carolinas. Park admission runs about $8 for adults.

Know before you go: no public swim area on either lake has lifeguards, fees and hours change seasonally, and Keowee's bottom can drop from wading depth to 40+ feet surprisingly close to shore — check the 3D depth map to see where. Water levels move too; today's reading is on the Lake Level page.

Quick answers

Where can you swim on Lake Keowee?

High Falls County Park (buoyed swim area), Mile Creek County Park (warm shallow beach), and South Cove County Park (popular beach, not formally designated) are the main public spots. Keowee-Toxaway State Park offers natural-shoreline swimming.

Does Lake Keowee have public beaches?

Yes — sandy beaches at High Falls, Mile Creek, and South Cove county parks. All swim-at-your-own-risk, all with small per-vehicle fees.

Can you swim in Lake Jocassee?

Absolutely — it's the clearest water in the state. Get in through Devils Fork State Park and swim from the small shoreline beaches, or off a boat or paddleboard on the open lake.
